# PiP Subtitles — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-11

PiP Subtitles does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any user data.

- All processing (video detection, subtitle capture, rendering) happens locally in your
  browser on the page you activate the extension on.
- The only data the extension persists is your subtitle appearance preferences (font
  size, colors, position), stored in Chrome's extension sync storage and accessible only
  to you and the extension.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics,
  telemetry, or remote code.
